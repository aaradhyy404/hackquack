How it works

1. Add your profile

Education, marks, income and category. No Aadhaar or bank details.

2. See your matches

Fully matched, potential match or not eligible, with reasons.

3. Save and prepare

Save schemes, tick documents and set reminders.

4. Apply officially

Go to the official portal. The authority makes the final decision.

Scholix is an information tool, not an official portal. Scholarship records are unverified sample data; confirm every detail on scholarships.gov.in or the scheme’s official site.
